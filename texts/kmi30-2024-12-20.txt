                            RE-COMPOSITION OF KSE-MEEZAN 30 INDEX (KMI-30)
It is hereby informed that the Pakistan Stock Exchange has carried out the re-composition exercise of KMI-
30 for the review period from January 1, 2024 to June 30, 2024.
The re-composition has been carried out on the basis of the selection criteria as detailed in the brochure of
KMI 30 Index, which is available on the Exchange’s website. (www.psx.com.pk)
As a result of this exercise, following are the incoming and outgoing constituents from the index:

            S.No.                   Incoming                                                 Outgoing
              1        Oil & Gas Development Co. limited                        Cherat Cement Company Limited^
              2             Sazgar Engineering Works                                    Dolmen City REIT^
              3               Pak Elektron Limited                                      Interloop Limited^
              4        Honda Atlas Cars (Pakistan) Limited                        NetSol Technologies Limited^
              5                Fauji Foods Limited                                    Nishat Mills Limited^
              6                 K-Electric Limited                                   Shell Pakistan Limited^
              7       Dawood Hercules Corporation Limited                            TPL Properties Limited^
          ^ Not included in top 30 rank



The list of final index constituents and the total pool of KMI-30 Shariah compliant companies are enclosed
with this notice. The recomposed index will be implemented w.e.f. Monday, December 30, 2024.
Note: In the event of ENGRO’s trading suspension in accordance with the Exchange’s regulations and Notice issued, followed by its delisting due
to the scheme of arrangement with DAWH, the next eligible company in the ranking will be added to the KMI-30 Index as per the Index rules
and approval from the Index Committee, with the treatment of DAWH’s weight determined based on the scheme of arrangement and Index
rules.
