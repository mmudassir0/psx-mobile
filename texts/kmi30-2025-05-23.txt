                         RE-COMPOSITION OF KSE-MEEZAN 30 INDEX (KMI30)
It is hereby informed that the Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited has carried out the re-composition exercise
of KMI30 for the review period from July 01, 2024 to December 31, 2024.
The re-composition has been carried out on the basis of the selection criteria as detailed in the brochure of
KMI 30 Index, which is available on the Exchange’s website (www.psx.com.pk)
As a result of this exercise, following are the incoming and outgoing constituents from the index:
           S.No.                INCOMING                                             OUTGOING
             1      Ghandhara Automobiles Limited                  Air Link Communication Limited^^

             5      Avanceon Limited                               Unity Foods Limited^
       Note: ^ stock did not fall within the top 30 ranked Shariah-compliant companies based on the index methodology
             ^^ stock has been excluded due to its Shariah non-compliance status as per the latest screening



The list of final index constituents and the total pool of KMI30 Shariah compliant companies are enclosed
with this notice. The recomposed index will be implemented w.e.f. Monday, June 02, 2025.
