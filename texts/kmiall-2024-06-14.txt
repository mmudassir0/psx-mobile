                                                                    NOTICE

PSX/N-576                                                                         Dated: June 14, 2024

                    RECOMPOSITION OF PSX-KMI ALL SHARE ISLAMIC INDEX
It is hereby informed that the Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited has carried out the re-composition exercise
of PSX-KMI ALL SHARE ISLAMIC INDEX for the review period July 01, 2023 to December 31, 2023. The re-
composition has been carried out on the basis of the criteria of selection of companies as detailed in the
brochure of PSX-KMI All Share Islamic Index, which can be downloaded from the website of the Exchange:
www.psx.com.pk.
The recomposed PSX-KMI All Share Islamic Index will comprise of 255 companies, in aggregate, out of which
28 new companies are included in the Index (including 5 newly listed companies in 2024), whereas, 29
companies have been removed from the Index. Enclosed herewith is the list with complete details of
inclusion and exclusion of the recomposed Index.
The recomposed PSX-KMI All Share Index will be implemented w.e.f. Tuesday, June 25, 2024.
