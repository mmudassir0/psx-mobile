PSX/N-610                                                                                 May 15, 2026

                      RE-COMPOSITION OF KSE-MEEZAN 30 INDEX (KMI30)
It is hereby notified that Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited has completed the re-composition exercise of the
KMI-30 Index, based on the Shariah status of listed companies as provided by the Exchange’s partner
Shariah Advisor and duly approved by the Index Committee, using accounts as of December 31, 2025.
The re-composition has been undertaken in accordance with the selection criteria detailed in the KMI-30
Index brochure, available on the Exchange’s website (www.psx.com.pk).
