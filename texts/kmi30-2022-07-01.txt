                 NOTICE FOR ALL MARKET PARTICIPANTS
      Subject: RE-COMPOSITION OF KSE-MEEZAN 30 INDEX (KMI-30)

It is hereby informed that the Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited has carried-out the exercise of re-
composition of KSE-Meezan 30 Index (KMI 30 Index) for the review period from July 01, 2021 to December
31, 2021.

The re-composition has been carried out on the basis of the selection criteria as detailed in the Brochure of
KMI 30 Index, which is available at the Exchange website (www.psx.com.pk).
As a result of re-composition, 5 companies would be affected as given hereunder: -
                                                                                         Exclusion
 Sr.no                   Incoming                                   Outgoing              Reason
   1       Ghani Global Holdings Limited                  Fauji Cement Co. Limited       Rank#33*
   2       Treet Corporation Limited                      Ghandhara Industries Limited   Rank#35*
   3       Telecard Limited                               Pak Elektron Limited           Rank#36*
   4       Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited             International Steels Limited   Rank#37*
   5       Dawood Hercules Corporation Limited            Azgard Nine Limited            Rank#38*
 * Does not fall in list of top 30 Shariah Compliant stocks


List of companies included in the recomposed Index and the list of sharia screen companies are also attached.

The recomposed index will be implemented w.e.f. Friday, 8th of July, 2022.
