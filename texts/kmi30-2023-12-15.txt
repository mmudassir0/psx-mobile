                                                             NOTICE
PSX/N-1280                                                                              December 15, 2023



               RE-COMPOSITION OF KSE-MEEZAN 30 INDEX (KMI-30)

It is hereby informed that the Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited has carried-out the exercise of re-
composition of KSE-Meezan 30 Index (KMI-30) for the review period from January 01, 2023 to June 30,
2023.
The re-composition has been carried out on the basis of the selection criteria as detailed in the brochure of
KMI 30 Index, which is available on the Exchange’s website (www.psx.com.pk).
As a result of this exercise, following are the incoming and outgoing companies from the index:

                          Incoming                                        Outgoing
           Colgate-Palmolive (Pakistan) Limited          Dawood Hercules Corporation Limited^
           The Searle Company Limited                    NetSol Technologies Limited^^
           Cnergyico PK Limited                          Pak Elektron Limited^^
           Dolmen City REIT                              Shell Pakistan Limited^^
         ^Does not meet Shariah Criteria of KMI-30
         ^^ Not included in top 30 rank

The list of final index constituents and the total pool of KMI-30 Shariah compliant companies are enclosed with this
notice.

The recomposed index will be implemented w.e.f. Friday, December 22, 2023.
