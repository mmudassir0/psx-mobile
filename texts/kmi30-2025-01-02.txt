SPECIAL RE-COMPOSITION OF KSE-100, KSE30, KMI30 AND PSXDIV20 INDEX

                                                                                                                   NOTICE
PSX/N-019                                                                                                                           January 02, 2025
                 SPECIAL RE-COMPOSITION OF KSE-100, KSE30, KMI30 & PSXDIV20 INDEX
With reference to PSX Notice PSX/N-1257 dated December 20, 2024, it is hereby informed that the Pakistan Stock
Exchange Limited has carried out a special re-composition exercise of KSE-100, KSE30, KMI30 & PSXDIV20 Indices,
following the Scheme of Arrangement regarding restructuring of Engro Corporation Limited (ENGRO) and Dawood
Hercules Corporation (DAWH), in accordance with the relevant index rules available on PSX website
(https://www.psx.com.pk).


As a result, ENGRO is being replaced by the stock mentioned below for each respective index:


     S.No.            Index Name                                  Outgoing                                            Incoming
       1                KSE-100                                                                              Kohinoor Textile Mills Limited
       2                 KSE-30                     Engro Corporation Limited                             Dawood Hercules Corporation Limited
       3                KMI-30                                                                               Ghandhara Industries Limited
       4               PSXDIV20                                                                           Dawood Hercules Corporation Limited
Constituent’s weight in the respective indices will be revised as per index rules, past practices and nature of event.




The above changes in the Indices will be implemented w.e.f. Monday, January 06, 2025.
