                        RE-COMPOSITION OF KSE-MEEZAN 30 INDEX (KMI-30)
It is hereby informed that the Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited has carried-out the exercise of re-
composition of KSE-Meezan 30 Index (KMI-30) for the review period from July 1, 2023 to December 31,
2023.
The re-composition has been carried out on the basis of the selection criteria as detailed in the brochure of
KMI 30 Index, which is available on the Exchange’s website (www.psx.com.pk).
As a result of this exercise, following are the incoming and outgoing companies from the index:

   S.No                      Incoming                                    Outgoing
     1        Shell (Pakistan) Limited              Attock Petroleum Limited^
     2        Nishat Mills Limited                  Avanceon Limited^
     3        Interloop Limited                     Cnergyico PK Limited^
     4        NetSol Technologies Limited           Colgate-Palmolive (Pakistan) Limited^^
     5        Faysal Bank Limited                   Lotte Chemical Pakistan Limited^
     6        AirLink Communication Limited         Oil & Gas Development Company Limited^^
^ Not included in top 30 rank
^^ Does not meet Shariah Criteria of KMI-30

The list of final index constituents and the total pool of KMI-30 Shariah compliant companies are enclosed
with this notice. The recomposed index will be implemented w.e.f. Friday, June 14, 2024.
