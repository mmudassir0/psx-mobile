NOTICE FOR ALL MARKET PARTICIPANTS - RE-COMPOSITION OF KSE-MEEZAN 30 INDEX (KMI-30)

                                                                  NOTICE

PSX/N- 1302                                                                           Dated: December 23, 2022

                 NOTICE FOR ALL MARKET PARTICIPANTS
      Subject: RE-COMPOSITION OF KSE-MEEZAN 30 INDEX (KMI-30)

It is hereby informed that the Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited has carried out the exercise of re-
composition of KSE-Meezan 30 Index (KMI 30 Index) for the review period from January 01, 2022 to June
30, 2022.

The re-composition has been carried out on the basis of the selection criteria as detailed in the Brochure of
KMI 30 Index, which is available on the Exchange website (www.psx.com.pk).
As a result of re-composition, 3 companies would be affected as given hereunder: -
    Sr.no                 Incoming                              Outgoing                   Exclusion Reason
      1          TPL Properties Limited             Hub Power Company Limited.             Non-compliant *
      2          Fauji Cement Co. Limited           Sui Northern Gas Pipe Line Ltd.               **
      3          Pak Elektron Limited               International Industries Ltd.                 **
 * Shariah Non-Compliant
 ** Does not fall in list of top 30 Shariah Compliant stocks




List of companies included in the recomposed Index and the list of sharia screen companies are also attached.

The recomposed index will be implemented w.e.f. Friday, 30th of December, 2022.
